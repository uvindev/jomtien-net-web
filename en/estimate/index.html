Scoping questions

What will your project actually take?

Six questions. At the end you get a written summary of what you described and a considered estimate from a person who has read it.

You will not get an instant number. An exact price needs a defined scope, and six questions cannot define your scope — anyone who gives you a figure at this stage is guessing, and you will pay for the guess later.
